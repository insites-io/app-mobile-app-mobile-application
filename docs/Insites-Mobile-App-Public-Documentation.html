1. Introduction

The Insites Mobile Application is a cross-platform mobile app built for Android and iOS devices. It provides users with a curated collection of cocktail recipes, allowing them to browse, create, and manage recipes from anywhere.

Key capabilities of the application include:

  • Recipe Management — Browse, create, edit, and view detailed cocktail recipes with images, ingredients, and step-by-step instructions.
  • Favorites — Save your preferred recipes locally for quick access.
  • Notifications — Stay informed when new recipes are added to the collection.
  • Profile Management — Update your personal details, profile picture, and password.
  • Secure Authentication — Create an account with email verification and sign in securely.
Supported Platforms: Android (API 21+) and iOS (14.0+). The app requires an active internet connection to load and manage recipes.

3. Authentication

The Insites Mobile Application uses secure token-based authentication. All user sessions are encrypted and stored securely on your device.

3.1 Creating an Account

To create a new account, follow these steps:

  1. From the Welcome Screen, tap "CREATE AN ACCOUNT".
  2. Fill in the required fields:
    • First Name — Your first name.
    • Last Name — Your last name.
    • Email — A valid email address (e.g., user@example.com). This will be used for sign-in and verification.
    • Password — Choose a secure password.
    • Confirm Password — Re-enter your password to confirm it matches.
  3. Tap "CREATE AN ACCOUNT" to submit the registration form.
  4. You will be redirected to the Email Verification screen.
Screenshot: Sign Up Screen Add an annotated screenshot of the Sign Up form showing all required fields and the "CREATE AN ACCOUNT" button.
Form Validation: All fields are required. The email must be in a valid format, and both password fields must match. If any validation fails, error messages will appear below the relevant field.

3.2 Email Verification

After creating an account, you must verify your email address before signing in.

  1. On the Verify Your Email screen, your registered email address is displayed.
  2. Tap "SEND VERIFICATION EMAIL" to receive a verification link in your inbox.
  3. Open the email and click the verification link.
  4. Return to the app and tap "I'VE ALREADY VERIFIED" to proceed to the Sign In screen.
Screenshot: Verify Email Screen Add an annotated screenshot showing the email display, "SEND VERIFICATION EMAIL" button, and "I'VE ALREADY VERIFIED" button.
Resend Cooldown: After sending a verification email, a 30-second cooldown applies before you can request another one. The button displays a countdown timer during this period.
Important: If you attempt to sign in before verifying your email, you will be redirected back to the Verify Email screen.

3.3 Signing In

To sign in to your account:

  1. From the Welcome Screen, tap "SIGN IN".
  2. Enter your registered Email and Password.
  3. Tap "SIGN IN" to authenticate.
  4. Upon successful authentication, you will be taken to the Home Screen.
Screenshot: Sign In Screen Add an annotated screenshot of the Sign In form showing the Email field, Password field, "Forgot Password" link, and "SIGN IN" button.

If your credentials are incorrect, an error banner will appear at the top of the form describing the issue.

Session Persistence: Once signed in, the app remembers your session. You will not need to sign in again unless you explicitly sign out or your session expires.

3.4 Forgot Password

If you have forgotten your password, tap the "Forgot Password" link on the Sign In screen. This feature allows you to reset your password via email.

Note: The Forgot Password flow will redirect you to an external password reset page where you can set a new password.

The top section includes:

  • The Insites logo in the header area.
  • A prompt: "What are you looking for?"
  • A search bar for searching cocktail recipes.
  • A menu icon to open the Category Drawer.

A prominent promotional banner is displayed below the search bar. The default banner reads "Cocktail Party - We got you!" with a "SEE ALL RECIPES" call-to-action button that navigates to the full Cocktails List.

This section showcases the top 4 highest-rated cocktail recipes. Recipes are sorted by rating in descending order. Each recipe is displayed as a card with:

4.4 Category Drawer

Tap the menu icon in the top-right corner of the header to open the Category Drawer. The drawer slides in from the right and provides quick navigation to recipe categories such as:

5. Recipes

The Recipes feature is the core of the Insites Mobile Application. It allows you to browse the full collection of cocktail recipes, view detailed preparation instructions, and contribute your own recipes.

5.1 Browsing Recipes

The Cocktails List screen displays every recipe in the database. You can reach this screen from:

  • The "SEE ALL RECIPES" button on the Home Screen promotional banner.
  • The "SEE ALL" link on any section header (Most Popular, Newly Added).
  • The Category Drawer by selecting the Cocktails category.

Each recipe in the list is displayed as a card showing:

Element Description
Image A 16:10 aspect ratio image of the cocktail. A placeholder icon is shown if no image is available.
Name The name of the cocktail recipe.
Rating A star icon with a numeric rating (e.g., 4.5) if available.
Screenshot: Cocktails List Screen Add an annotated screenshot of the Cocktails List showing the recipe cards with image, name, and rating elements highlighted.

Pull to Refresh

Pull down on the list to refresh the recipe data from the server. The list will reload with the latest recipes.

5.2 Viewing Recipe Details

Tap any recipe card to open the Cocktail Detail screen. This screen provides a comprehensive view of the recipe, structured as follows:

Hero Image

A full-width image is displayed at the top of the screen. Navigation controls are overlaid on the image:

  • Back arrow (top-left) — Returns to the previous screen.
  • Edit icon (top-right) — Opens the recipe in edit mode.

Recipe Information

Below the hero image, the following details are displayed:

Field Description
Category Badge A label indicating the recipe type (e.g., "Cocktail").
Rating A star icon with the numeric rating value.
Name The cocktail name in bold heading text.
Favorites Toggle "ADD TO FAVORITES" / "REMOVE FAVORITE" link with a heart icon.
Duration Preparation time in minutes (e.g., "Duration: 10 minutes").
Amount Number of servings (e.g., "Amount: 2 servings").

Tabbed Content

Recipe content is organized into two tabs:

  • Ingredients — A numbered list of all ingredients required for the recipe.
  • Instructions — Step-by-step preparation instructions.

Tap either tab to switch between Ingredients and Instructions. The active tab is highlighted with a pink accent color.

Leave a Review

At the bottom of the screen, a "LEAVE A REVIEW" button allows you to submit feedback about the recipe.

Screenshot: Cocktail Detail Screen Add an annotated screenshot showing the hero image, recipe information, tab bar (Ingredients/Instructions), and "LEAVE A REVIEW" button.

5.3 Adding a New Recipe

To add a new cocktail recipe, tap the center "+" button on the bottom navigation bar. This opens the Add Recipe screen.

Complete the following fields:

Field Required Description
Name Yes The name of the cocktail recipe.
Keywords No Tags to categorize the recipe. Type a keyword and tap the add button to create a tag chip. Tap the "x" on any chip to remove it.
Duration (min) No Preparation time in minutes (numeric value).
Amount (servings) No Number of servings the recipe yields (numeric value).
Image No Upload a photo of the finished cocktail from your device gallery.
Ingredients No List of ingredients, one per line.
Instructions No Step-by-step preparation instructions.
Screenshot: Add Recipe Screen Add an annotated screenshot of the Add Recipe form showing the Name field, Keywords chips, Duration/Amount fields, Image picker, Ingredients, and Instructions text areas.

Submitting the Recipe

  1. Fill in the recipe details. At minimum, a Name is required.
  2. Optionally, tap the image area to select a photo from your gallery.
  3. Tap "SAVE" to submit the recipe.
  4. A success message will appear confirming the recipe was added. You will be redirected to the Cocktails List.

Tap "CANCEL" to clear the form and start over.

Image Upload: Images are automatically optimized (max 1200px, 85% quality) before uploading to keep file sizes manageable.

5.4 Editing a Recipe

To edit an existing recipe:

  1. Navigate to the recipe's Detail Screen.
  2. Tap the edit icon (pencil) in the top-right corner of the hero image.
  3. The Edit Recipe screen will open with all fields pre-populated with the current recipe data.
  4. Make your changes and tap "UPDATE" to save.
  5. A success message will confirm the update, and you will be returned to the Detail Screen with the refreshed data.

Tap "CANCEL" to discard changes and return to the Detail Screen.

Screenshot: Edit Recipe Screen Add an annotated screenshot of the Edit Recipe form showing the pre-populated fields and the "UPDATE" and "CANCEL" buttons.

5.5 Recipe Images

Recipe images are stored in the cloud and loaded dynamically. When adding or editing a recipe:

  • Tap the image picker area to select a photo from your device gallery.
  • A preview of the selected image is shown before submission.
  • When editing, the existing image is displayed. Selecting a new image will replace it upon saving.
  • If no image is provided, a placeholder icon is displayed in the recipe card and detail view.

6. Favorites

The Favorites feature allows you to bookmark your preferred cocktail recipes for quick access. Favorites are stored locally on your device and are tied to your user account.

6.1 Adding to Favorites

You can add a recipe to your favorites from the Cocktail Detail screen:

  1. Open any recipe's Detail Screen.
  2. Look for the "ADD TO FAVORITES" text with a heart icon, located next to the recipe name.
  3. Tap it to add the recipe to your favorites. The heart icon will turn red and the text will change to "REMOVE FAVORITE".

6.2 Viewing Your Favorites

Tap the heart icon (4th tab) on the bottom navigation bar to view all your saved favorites.

Your favorites are displayed in a 2-column grid layout. Each card shows the recipe image, name, and rating. Tap any card to open its Detail Screen.

Screenshot: Favorites Tab Add an annotated screenshot of the Favorites Tab showing the grid layout with recipe cards. Include a second screenshot of the empty state showing the "No favorites added" message and "ADD FAVORITES" button.

Empty State

If you have not added any favorites yet, the screen displays:

  • A heading: "No favorites added"
  • A message: "There are no recipes added in your favorites."
  • An "ADD FAVORITES" button that navigates to the Cocktails List so you can browse and add recipes.

6.3 Removing a Favorite

To remove a recipe from your favorites:

  1. Open the recipe's Detail Screen.
  2. Tap the "REMOVE FAVORITE" text (displayed when the recipe is already favorited).
  3. The heart icon will revert to an outline, and the recipe will be removed from your Favorites tab.

7. Notifications

The Notifications feature keeps you informed about new content added to the recipe collection. Tap the bell icon (2nd tab) on the bottom navigation bar to access your notifications.

7.1 Notification Types

Type Trigger Description
New Recipe A new cocktail recipe is added to the database Notifies you that a new recipe is available, including the recipe name in the notification body.

Each notification displays:

  • Title — A brief heading (e.g., "New cocktail added").
  • Body — A description with details about the new content.
  • Timestamp — A relative time indicator (e.g., "5m ago", "2h ago", "Yesterday").
  • Read/Unread Status — Unread notifications appear in bold text.
Screenshot: Notifications Tab Add an annotated screenshot of the Notifications Tab showing a list of notifications with read/unread states and timestamps. Include an annotation showing the swipe gesture directions.

7.2 Managing Notifications

Notifications can be managed using swipe gestures:

Gesture Action Visual Indicator
Swipe left to right Toggle read/unread status Green background ("Mark as Read") or yellow background ("Mark as Unread")
Swipe right to left Delete the notification Red background with "Delete" text

Empty State

If there are no notifications, the screen displays a centered message: "No notifications yet".

The following fields are available for editing:

Field Description

Partial Updates: Only fields that differ from your current data will be sent to the server. You can update a single field without affecting others.

8.2 Profile Picture

To upload or change your profile picture:

  1. On the My Details screen, tap the circular profile picture area or the "UPLOAD IMAGE" button.
  2. Choose your image source:
    • Camera — Take a new photo using your device camera.
    • Gallery — Select an existing photo from your device.
  3. A preview of the selected image will appear in the circular area.
  4. Tap "UPDATE MY DETAILS" to upload and save the new profile picture.

8.3 Change Password

To change your password:

  1. Navigate to Profile > Settings > Change Password.
  2. Enter your new password in the "New Password" field.
  3. Use the eye icon to toggle password visibility if needed.
  4. Tap "CHANGE PASSWORD" to submit.
Screenshot: Change Password Screen Add an annotated screenshot of the Change Password screen showing the password field, visibility toggle, and action buttons.
Automatic Sign-Out: You will be automatically signed out after successfully changing your password. You will need to sign in again with your new password.

8.4 Privacy Policy

Tap "Privacy Policy" under the About Insites section to read the application's privacy policy. This screen displays the full privacy policy text in a scrollable view.

8.5 Terms & Conditions

Tap "Terms & Conditions" under the About Insites section to read the application's terms of use. This screen displays the full terms and conditions text in a scrollable view.

Legal Acknowledgment: By creating an account and using the app, you agree to the Terms and Privacy policies. Links to these documents are also available on the Welcome Screen.

8.6 Signing Out

To sign out of your account:

  1. Navigate to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap the "Log Out" button (displayed in red text).
  3. A confirmation dialog will appear asking: "Are you sure you want to sign out?"
  4. Tap "Sign Out" to confirm, or "Cancel" to stay signed in.
  5. After signing out, you will be redirected to the Welcome Screen.
Data Persistence: Signing out clears your authentication session. Your favorites and notifications are stored per-account and will be restored when you sign back in on the same device.

10. Troubleshooting

Issue Solution
Cannot sign in
  • Verify your email address is correct.
  • Check that your email has been verified (see Section 3.2).
  • Try resetting your password using the "Forgot Password" link.
  • Ensure you have an active internet connection.
Verification email not received
  • Check your spam/junk folder.
  • Wait for the 30-second cooldown, then tap "RESEND VERIFICATION EMAIL".
  • Ensure the email address you registered with is correct.
Recipes not loading
  • Check your internet connection.
  • Pull down on the list to trigger a manual refresh.
  • Close and reopen the app.
Image upload fails
  • Ensure you have granted the app permission to access your photos.
  • Check your internet connection. Image uploads require a stable connection.
  • Try selecting a smaller image.
Favorites not showing
  • Favorites are stored locally per device. If you recently reinstalled the app or cleared app data, your favorites will need to be re-added.
Signed out after changing password
  • This is expected behavior. After changing your password, you are automatically signed out for security. Sign in with your new password.
App crashing or unresponsive
  • Force close the app and reopen it.
  • Ensure your device OS is updated to a supported version.
  • Clear the app cache from your device settings.
  • Reinstall the app if the issue persists.
